About me

A programmer with
a broader view.

I enjoy working across a game: building its mechanics, shaping how enemies behave, and finding ways to make the experience work better for players.

My background spans web development, Unity and Unreal Engine. My game development work includes enemy AI, boss fights, combat, vehicles, physics, VR interaction and multiplayer, alongside tools and supporting technology.

I contribute to both implementation and gameplay design, and have led development of a four-player VR game. I enjoy collaborating with designers, proposing ideas and solving problems that emerge during development. I’m based in Worthing, West Sussex, and open to hybrid and on-site roles in the UK, as well as remote roles worldwide.
