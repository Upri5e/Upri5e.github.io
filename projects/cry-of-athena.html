Overview

AI, combat and physical gameplay.

My work on Cry of Athena spans AI, vehicles, combat and sandbox features. I previously contributed as an Unreal Engine consultant, and my more recent work with the same team includes a physical catapult built around Unreal Engine’s physics constraints.

01

Constraint-based catapult

I worked on the catapult’s physical mechanism using Unreal Engine physics constraints. This contribution focuses on creating a gameplay object whose movement is driven by a constrained physical assembly.

02

Enemy and vehicle AI

I used Unreal Engine Behaviour Trees and EQS to implement hiding, player targeting and pursuit behaviours, including dynamic movement adjustments for AI vehicles.

03

Vehicles and bullet interactions

I worked with Unreal Engine’s vehicle system on two-, four- and six-plus-wheeled vehicles. I also implemented bullet interactions that allow projectiles to penetrate, deflect or be destroyed on contact with environmental surfaces.

04

Sandbox persistence and editing

I developed C++ save/load functionality to serialise player-created buildings and creations, alongside custom editor tools to support sandbox content creation.
