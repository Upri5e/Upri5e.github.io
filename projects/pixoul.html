Overview

AI, encounters and hands-on design.

At RobocomVR, I contributed to ten shipped location-based VR titles for Pixoul Planet. My work included enemy behaviours, boss encounters and vehicle combat. I also led development of the four-player Pixoul Recon and proposed design changes to make its combat work within the environment.

01

Enemy AI and behaviours

I created humanoid enemies with different weapons, two-axis turrets and spiders with custom behaviours, including an approved design I proposed where spiders jump onto the player’s face. I also built varied cover interactions: ducking, vaulting over cover and sliding underneath it.

02

Battle Rush

I built the multi-stage train boss, where players defeat enemies and destroy each train section to advance to the next. I also implemented AI pursuit and vehicle-bashing mechanics.

03

Pixoul Recon

I led development of this four-player game. To address difficult long-range combat in its large, dark environments, I proposed containing encounters within individual rooms and moving the players’ platform between them, preserving the scenery while making combat more manageable. I also integrated ProTube VR guns and simulator machines through their APIs and network connections.

04

Player interaction and weapons

Across the Pixoul titles, I implemented VR player inverse kinematics, character-scale calibration, weapon recoil and attachments that change weapon behaviour.
