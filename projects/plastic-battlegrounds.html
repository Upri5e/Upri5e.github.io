Overview

From online play to hands-on gameplay.

On Plastic Battlegrounds VR, I handled the multiplayer and online-services implementation alongside a broad range of gameplay systems. My work connects networked play with player data, developer-configured content, progression, teams and physics-based vehicles.

01

Multiplayer and online services

I handled the full multiplayer session flow and EOS integration, including lobbies, sessions, lobby-to-game travel and EOS Player Data Storage. I also worked on the conversion to a listen-server architecture, replicated gameplay and VR interactions, plus custom proximity and team voice functionality.

02

Data-driven spawning and unlocks

I built a system that turns developer-configured spawnable entities, settings and customisations into entries in the player’s spawning menu. Players can use that menu to spawn entities, while the unlock system saves and loads their unlocked spawnables so the available selection persists between sessions.

03

A reusable team system

I reworked the team system around a component that can be used on different entity types. Team data is defined in data tables, and interface calls let each entity respond to team changes. This makes the system scalable without binding team behaviour to one specific character or actor class.

04

Vehicle and tank gameplay

The custom vehicle system is part of Plastic Battlegrounds. I built component-based wheel physics, differential tank steering and weapon gameplay, with slope detection and optional gravity compensation for driving on steep inclines. Read the vehicle systems breakdown →

05

Cosmetics and store systems

My work also includes cosmetic equipment, its replication, and store integration. Developer-facing configuration supports reusable systems while allowing project-specific content and customisation.
