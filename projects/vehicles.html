Overview

Gameplay built on physics.

I developed this vehicle system for Plastic Battlegrounds VR. Custom wheel components apply forces to a shared vehicle body, while a wheel container tracks the arrangement defined by each vehicle class. The system also supports tank steering and configurable handling on steep terrain. View the full Plastic Battlegrounds project →

01

Component-based vehicles

Vehicle child classes define their wheel arrangement using custom wheel components. The shared vehicle logic discovers those wheels through their container, while each wheel contributes its own force to the body.

02

Tank steering

I extended the approach for tanks, where steering comes from the relative drive on the left and right sides rather than turning the wheels. Tank-specific gameplay lives on a base tank pawn.

03

Weapon gameplay

The tank base includes shooting functionality. This brings vehicle movement and combat together while leaving individual vehicle classes room to define their own setup.

04

Slope detection and gravity compensation

I added slope detection with an option to counter gravity on inclines. This supports vehicles that need to keep driving on steep surfaces without sliding down or falling away, allowing their handling to be configured around the intended gameplay.
